Our policy

Sales & Holds

Thank you for following Taaffe Vintage. Every piece is vintage and sold as-is, with normal signs of wear and use that come with age — scratches, scuffs, stains, and chips can be present. Major damage is disclosed in the listing, but not every flaw is easily photographed. Please ask for additional photos or details before purchasing if you have any questions. All sales are final.

Items are listed across multiple platforms (Instagram, Facebook Marketplace, and others) and sold first-come, first-served. Availability is not guaranteed until a deposit or full payment has been received.

Pieces cannot be held without a non-refundable deposit of 50% of the purchase price. Once paid in full, items must be picked up or have delivery arranged within 30 days. Items not picked up within 30 days will be relisted, and deposits are not refunded.

Delivery & Pickup

Pickup is available by appointment in Kingwood, TX (NE Houston area).

Local curbside delivery is available for an additional cost based on mileage. To arrange delivery, we'll first confirm the exact delivery address, agreed-upon date and time, and final delivery cost. After the delivery plan is confirmed, a 50% non-refundable deposit on the piece is required to lock in the schedule. The remaining balance is due upon delivery.

Confirming the exact address before quoting protects both of us — quotes are based on actual mileage, not approximate location.

If you arrange a third-party transport service (whether recommended by us or arranged by you), full payment for the piece is required before the item leaves our possession. The transporter is hired and paid directly by you. Any damage during pickup, transit, or drop-off is between you and the transporter. Taaffe Vintage assumes no liability for items once they leave our location with a third party.

Payment

We accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, and Cash App. Standard "friends and family" or peer-to-peer transfers carry no fee on either side.

If you prefer a protected payment option (PayPal Goods & Services, Venmo Goods & Services, or any payment method that includes purchase protection), we ask that you cover the associated processing fee. This keeps pricing fair for buyers who pay with no-fee options.

Thank you for supporting our small business.
