The origin story

A cheap dresser and an expensive quote.

I bought a dresser at Goodwill and took it to a refinisher, and he wanted over a thousand dollars to bring it back.

I couldn't afford that. So I bought sandpaper instead.

— That dresser is still in my house.

I'm an engineer who can't stop rescuing things.

By day I'm a mechanical engineer for a geothermal company. Nights and weekends, I'm in the garage or out at the barn. Taaffe Vintage is what happened when I realized I couldn't keep every piece I refinished — the house, thankfully, has limits.

What it doesn't have limits on is the standard of the work. Every joint gets checked. Every drawer glides. Every finish is chosen for the piece, not the trend. The pieces other people give up on are usually the ones most worth saving — and that's the whole thing, really.

why I do this

The landfill gets enough from us already.

Fast furniture has trained a generation to throw out something broken and buy something cheap to replace it. An oak dresser from 1962 that needs two hours of sanding and a new set of drawer slides becomes garbage — while a particleboard replacement gets shipped in from overseas.

Most pieces are worth saving. That's the whole thing. I work on what other people have given up on, because the math on that keeps getting more obvious: old furniture is built better, lasts longer, and doesn't need to be replaced every five years. The only thing missing is someone with the time and the sandpaper.

Keep it out of the landfill

Every piece refinished is a piece not replaced. That's the quiet math behind the whole shop.

Slower than fast furniture

Hand sanding takes a weekend. Shipping from overseas takes an hour. We chose the wrong one as a culture.

Every sale supports the herd

A portion of each sale goes to feed, farrier, and vet care for the rescue horses. The work and the herd are one thing.

From overlooked find to someone's forever piece.

Step one

Hunt

I look for solid wood, dovetails, and good bones — not a name on the back.

Step two

Assess

Every drawer pulled, every joint checked, every veneer tested. What needs repair gets repaired.

Step three

Refinish

Strip. Sand. Repair. Stain or paint depending on the grain. Hardware cleaned or replaced to period-correct spec.

Step four

Rehome

Photographed in natural light, posted on Instagram, delivered by me in Houston — or shipped carefully beyond.

The other rescue

Retired. Not finished.

The other thing I rescue doesn't fit in a pickup truck. I rehabilitate older horses — most of them ex-show jumpers with injuries that ended their competitive careers. The ones other people saw as finished.

It turns out it's the same instinct as the furniture. Look past the damage. Notice the bones. Put in the time. Give something built for one life a shot at another one.

A portion of every sale goes toward feed, farrier visits, and vet care for the herd. You're not just buying a dresser.

“Nobody saved these horses, so I did. Same reason I started saving the furniture.”
Four generations deep

It turns out this runs in the family.

I didn't grow up being told I'd do this work. But the further I got into it, the more the family stories started to line up. It turns out refinishing furniture is something the Taaffes have been doing for a long time — I'm just the one doing it now.

Early 1900s
Great-Grandfather
Carpenter · Swedish immigrant
Left Sweden with his wife for America and brought his business with him. Trained as a carpenter, he laid the hardwood floors inside John Wayne's house — floors that are, as far as we know, still there.
Mid-century
My grandmother & her twin
Second generation
My grandmother's twin sister refinished furniture exactly the way I do now — the same steps, the same patience, decades before I was born. The tools stayed in the family even when the trade went quiet.
My childhood
My mother
Third generation · The curator
My mom thrifted pieces alongside my grandmother — a piece here, a different piece there, slowly curating a collection for her room. She painted instead of refinishing, but the eye was the same: spotting what was worth saving, knowing it would matter once it was hers.
Today
Brianna Byrd
Fourth generation · Founder
I picked up a piece of sandpaper because I couldn't afford a quote, not because I knew the history. Turns out I was following it anyway. I chose Taaffe — the original spelling of our family name — as a way of taking the name back to where it started.

Our policy

Sales & Holds

Thank you for following Taaffe Vintage. Our inventory consists of vintage, antique, or preowned pieces and will show signs of age, use, repair, wear, and imperfection consistent with their history. Scratches, scuffs, stains, chips, veneer repairs, and other signs of use may be present.

Descriptions, dimensions, wood species, age estimates, designer attributions, and condition notes reflect our general observations and research, but are not guaranteed. Listing photos and descriptions are provided as a general representation of the item and may not capture every detail, imperfection, repair, or sign of wear.

Buyers are responsible for reviewing all photos carefully, visiting the item in-person for an inspection, and requesting any additional information, measurements, photos, or videos prior to purchase if condition or suitability is a concern.

All items are sold as-is and all sales are final.

Items are listed across multiple platforms (Instagram, Facebook Marketplace, and others) and sold first-come, first-served. Availability is not guaranteed until a deposit or full payment has been received.

Pieces cannot be held without a non-refundable deposit of 50% of the purchase price. Once a deposit has been paid, items must be picked up or have delivery arranged within 30 days. Items not picked up within 30 days will be relisted, and deposits are not refunded.

Delivery & Pickup

Pickup is available by appointment in Kingwood, TX (NE Houston area).

Local curbside delivery is available for an additional cost based on mileage. To arrange delivery, we'll first confirm the exact delivery address, agreed-upon date and time, and final delivery cost. After the delivery plan is confirmed, a 50% non-refundable deposit on the piece is required to lock in the schedule. The remaining balance is due upon delivery.

Confirming the exact address before quoting protects both of us — quotes are based on actual mileage, not approximate location. We encourage a viewing in person before purchasing to ensure you are happy with the item, as all items are sold in as-is condition.

If you arrange a third-party transport service (whether recommended by us or arranged by you), full payment for the piece is required before the item leaves our possession. The transporter is hired and paid directly by you. Any damage during pickup, transit, or drop-off is between you and the transporter. Taaffe Vintage assumes no liability for items once they leave our possession.

Payment

We accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, Venmo, Zelle, PayPal, and Cash App. Standard "friends and family" or peer-to-peer transfers carry no fee on either side.

If you prefer a protected payment option (PayPal Goods & Services, Venmo Goods & Services, or any payment method that includes purchase protection), we ask that you cover the associated processing fee. This keeps pricing fair for buyers who pay with no-fee options.

Thank you for supporting our small business.
